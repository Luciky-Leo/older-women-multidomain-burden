# Novelty Matrix

## Bottom Line

The broad topic "international aging cohorts + frailty / biological age / ADL transition / sex differences" is crowded. The defensible angle is a women-only multidomain aging endotype study that avoids using a single frailty index or sex disparity as the main contribution.

Direct novelty query run on 2026-05-31:

No direct same-title/same-abstract collision was found in the first-pass web search, but adjacent literature is strong enough that novelty claims must stay conservative.

## Recommended Positioning

Use this exact study identity:

> A women-focused multidomain aging endotype study across seven international aging cohorts, integrating functional, cognitive, affective, and cardiometabolic trajectories and testing their associations with mortality and adverse health transitions.

Avoid these manuscript claims:

- "First cross-national frailty index."
- "First proof that women have higher frailty risk."
- "Biological age acceleration explains CVD risk."
- "Reproductive factors predict frailty in CHARLS."
- "ADL/IADL transition explains sex differences in life expectancy."

Use these safer claims after analysis, if supported:

- "Few studies have examined women-only multidomain aging endotypes across harmonized international aging cohorts."
- "This study shifts from single-domain frailty measures toward cross-domain aging patterning."
- "The analysis compares whether women-specific aging endotypes are reproducible across cohort systems and national contexts."
