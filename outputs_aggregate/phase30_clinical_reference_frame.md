# Phase 30 Clinical Reference Frame

Date: 2026-06-02

## Clinical positioning

- The manuscript should be framed around healthy ageing and functional ability, not around a generic prediction-score claim.
- The clinically meaningful construct is a four-domain pattern: functional limitation, cognition, affective symptoms and cardiometabolic or chronic-disease burden.
- The primary clinical endpoint should remain functional deterioration. Mortality is clinically important but secondary because proportional-hazards and piecewise diagnostics show instability in selected cohort-class terms.

## Evidence anchors used for manuscript revision

- WHO healthy-ageing framing: intrinsic capacity and functional ability are the natural clinical language for this study.
- Frailty phenotype and deficit-accumulation literature: supports why a single frailty/severity score is clinically familiar but can hide different domain patterns.
- ICOPE/intrinsic-capacity language: supports domain-oriented interpretation, while avoiding treatment recommendations from observational class membership.
- Current project validation: continuous four-domain score models often fit as well as or better than endotype-only models, so the claim is interpretable heterogeneity mapping, not universal prediction superiority.

## Main manuscript rule

Every clinical claim must pass three checks:

1. It is supported by a measured domain or outcome in the current data.
2. It is stated within cohort or as descriptive cross-cohort mapping, not as a pooled causal claim.
3. It does not imply a clinical intervention, diagnostic category or treatment assignment that was not tested.
